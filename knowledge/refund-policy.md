Customers may return an item within 30 days of purchase, provided they have
the receipt.[^refund-policy-interview-2026-09-02]

## Conditions

- The item must be unused.
- A receipt is required.
- The return must be made within 30 days of purchase.

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods are exempt from the 30-day window — a faulty item can be
returned outside that period.[^refund-policy-interview-2026-09-02]

Open question: whether faulty goods still need a receipt or must be unused
was not specified.

## Refund timing

Once a return is accepted, the refund lands within 5 working
days.[^refund-policy-interview-2026-09-02]
